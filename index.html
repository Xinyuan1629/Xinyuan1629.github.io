Hi, I’m Xinyuan.

Curious about the intelligence that emerges from learning.

I’m an undergraduate student in Applied Mathematics at Fudan University. My research interests lie in understanding why learning systems work, how they generalize, and how we can make them more reliable and accessible.

Recently, I’ve been exploring embodied intelligence, particularly how agents learn through interaction with the physical world.

In the long run, I hope to contribute to the foundations of AI and help turn advances in intelligence into technologies that truly benefit people beyond the research community.

Research Interests

Mathematical Foundations of AI

Understanding learning, optimization, generalization, and model behavior through principled mathematical approaches. I’m particularly interested in elegant formulations that reveal the mechanisms behind complex systems.

Embodied Learning & Spatial Intelligence

Exploring how agents learn from interaction, acquire spatial understanding, and adapt to uncertain environments, with a current focus on reinforcement learning and simulation-to-reality generalization.

Interpretable & Human-Centered AI

Making intelligent systems more understandable, controllable, and useful to people, informed by my previous work on medical AI systems, explainability, and interactive visualization.
